Privacy

Gmail

Connecting Gmail grants Vibecheck read access to your mailbox. The browser requests inbox subjects and senders for the picker. When you select Scan email, it retrieves that email and checks its text in your browser.

Vibecheck does not upload, store, share, or use Gmail data to train models. Google’s temporary access token stays in browser memory. Vibecheck makes no changes to your mailbox.

Disconnect clears the picker and asks Google to revoke access. You can also remove Vibecheck’s access from your Google Account connections.

Vibecheck’s use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Text and screenshots

The message scanner sends typed or pasted text to the Vibecheck server to calculate a score. Screenshot text extraction runs in your browser, and the resulting text is sent when you select Check the vibe. Vibecheck does not save those messages.
